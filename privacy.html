Privacy Policy

Xameran Content Studio · Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Scope

Xameran Content Studio ("the app") is a private desktop application used solely by Xameran to manage Xameran's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It has no other users.

2. Data the app accesses

After the account owner signs in and grants permission, the app accesses only data of that connected account:

  • YouTube: channel information and statistics, the account's own videos and their statistics, comments on those videos, YouTube Analytics reports for the channel, and the ability to upload videos.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar, username), profile statistics, the account's own videos and their statistics, and the ability to post videos to the account.

3. Use and storage

  • The data is used only to publish the account owner's own videos and to show how they perform.
  • All data stays on the account owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platforms' own APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train models.
  • This website and the app use no tracking, analytics or cookies.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the account is disconnected in the app, which deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in the Google account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in TikTok under "Manage app permissions". Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy.
