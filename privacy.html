Privacy Policy

The Xameran app is used only by Xameran for Xameran's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It accesses only the data of an account whose owner has signed in and granted permission.

That data stays on the owner's own device and is not sold, shared or used for advertising. This website uses no tracking or cookies.

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account permissions or in TikTok under "Manage app permissions". Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy.
